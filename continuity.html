Continuity Guidance

PLERA remains available as a public static reference even when a sovereign STOS runtime is offline. It does not replace the runtime, authenticate operators, recover secrets, or exercise operational authority.

Public boundary: this page intentionally contains no credentials, terminal tickets, private repository payloads, internal control endpoints, raw host diagnostics, or live operational health data.

When a local STOS runtime is unavailable

  1. Confirm the intended sovereign host is powered on and reachable by its authorized operator.
  2. Use the locally sealed commissioning and recovery records maintained by the operator; PLERA does not contain those private artifacts.
  3. Restore service through the approved local supervision chain rather than exposing a private Gateway directly to the public Internet.
  4. Verify integrity, supervision, and authority before resuming protected operations.

Recovery authority remains private

Routine administration and emergency recovery are deliberately separate. PLERA never requests or stores either form of authority. Any recovery credential, challenge, passcode, session, or control-plane secret belongs only to the sovereign environment that issued it.

Repository continuity

Public documentation may remain mirrored here for discoverability. Private repository material, repository-write authority, and materialization controls remain outside PLERA. Public copies are references, not the sovereign source of truth.

Public status interpretation

The accompanying status.json is sanitized commissioning metadata. It is not a live health feed and must not be used to infer whether a private STOS host, terminal, socket, or repository is currently online.
